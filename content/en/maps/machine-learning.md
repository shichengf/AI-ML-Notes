# Machine Learning Roadmap

## 1. Mathematical foundations

- Linear algebra: vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, and matrix decomposition
- Probability and statistics: random variables, common distributions, estimation, and hypothesis testing
- Calculus and optimization: gradients, the chain rule, and convex optimization

## 2. Classical machine learning

- Supervised learning: regression, classification, and model evaluation
- Unsupervised learning: clustering, dimensionality reduction, and representation learning
- Generalization: bias and variance, regularization, and cross validation

## 3. Deep learning

- Neural networks and backpropagation
- Optimizers, normalization, and regularization
- CNNs, Transformers, and generative models

## 4. AI engineering

- Data processing and experiment management
- Model deployment, monitoring, and MLOps
- Reliability, safety, and evaluation
